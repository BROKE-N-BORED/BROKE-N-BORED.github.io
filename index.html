Color Palettes — the website
This is your palette studio as a plain website. It lives in this browser — your palettes, stamps and paintings are saved on this device (browser storage), not inside any app. Add it to your home screen and it opens like an app.
App
In Chrome: tap the ⋮ menu (top right) → “Add to Home screen”. The palettes icon lands on your home screen and opens straight here.
